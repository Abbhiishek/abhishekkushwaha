## Maintenance rules

1. Keep URLs stable unless a redirect plan is part of the change.
2. Do not add personal incidents, production metrics, employers, or outcomes without repository evidence.
3. Prefer primary technical documentation for version-sensitive behavior.
4. Add a new row before publishing a new post; state what it owns and what neighboring posts must not repeat.
5. During rewrites, compare the thesis, opening problem, architecture, examples, and takeaway against this ledger.
